FAQ

What materials are the products made from?

Our collection celebrates natural clay and terracotta. Jewelry may also include thread, beads and metal fittings.

How do I care for clay products?

Gently wipe decorative pieces with a soft cloth and avoid harsh cleaners. Keep clay jewelry dry. Follow the maker’s care instructions for individual utensils.

How can I place an order?

Browse the collection, add your favorite pieces to the cart, and continue through checkout with your delivery details.

What are the shipping options?

Shipping locations, charges and delivery times will be available when the store opens.

How are the products made by artisans?

Artisans shape, dry, fire and finish the clay by hand. Each piece reflects the small variations that make handcraft special.
